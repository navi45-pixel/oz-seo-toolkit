Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about the free audit — the same ones our JSON API returns to AI assistants.

Is this website audit really free?

Yes — OzSEO Toolkit is 100% free with no sign-up and no card. Every audit runs all 76 checks on our servers at no cost.

What does the free audit check?

76 checks across 8 groups, covering:

  • On-page SEO (titles, meta, headings, Open Graph, internal links)
  • AI / GEO search visibility for ChatGPT, Copilot and Perplexity
  • Technical health (DNS, HTTPS, robots.txt, sitemap, 404 handling)
  • Speed and Core Web Vitals via Google PageSpeed
  • Security (SSL, headers, SPF/DMARC, mixed content)
  • Mobile readiness, content quality and Australian local SEO

What is GEO / AI search visibility?

GEO visibility is how easily AI assistants like ChatGPT, Copilot and Perplexity can find, understand and cite your business. The audit checks structured data, quotable answers and AI crawler access.

How long does an audit take?

Usually under 30 seconds. The audit fetches your page, runs DNS and security probes, and queries Google PageSpeed — all in one pass.

Do I need an account or API key?

No. The audit form is free for anyone, and even the JSON API needs no key.
