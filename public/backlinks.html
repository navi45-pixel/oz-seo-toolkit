Backlink safety tips

Quality beats quantity — one link from a real Australian site in your niche is worth 100 spam links.

✅ Do

Get listed in real Australian directories, earn quotes via journalist platforms, write guest posts for genuine AU blogs, sponsor local clubs/events, and keep your business Name-Address-Phone identical everywhere.

❌ Don't

Buy bulk "SEO backlink packages", join private link networks, spam blog comments, or use link farms. These trigger Google penalties and are a waste of money.

💡 AI-era tip

AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews) cite sites that are well-linked AND clearly described. Add FAQ content, schema markup, and get mentioned on authoritative Australian pages.
